# hiyo

An AI secretary with a sense of timing. A WHOOP strap streams live heart rate over Bluetooth LE. hiyo checks it against your calendar and preferences, then decides whether to stay quiet or call your phone for a natural two-way conversation.

Built for c0mpiled-16 at UC San Diego ("Invisible Interfaces").

> Not a medical device. hiyo makes no clinical claims and never contacts emergency services on its own. Calls go only to opted-in, allowlisted numbers.
